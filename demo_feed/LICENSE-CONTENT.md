# Demo Feed content

All Demo Feed content was created for the Sift project during the Meta Global AI Developer
Hackathon (Oct 27 to Nov 4, 2026) and is released under CC0 1.0 along with this repository.

- Post visuals are SVG drawn in the browser by `feed.js` from the text in `posts.js`.
- Reels in `media/` were rendered by `tools/make_reels.py` with ffmpeg (solid color + text) and
  narrated with the open-source Flite synthesizer (`slt` voice). No third-party footage, photos
  or music are used.
- All account names are fictional.
